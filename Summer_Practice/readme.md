## Topic Rules

You may choose one topic from the list, modify one, or propose your own topic for supervisor approval.

Your topic must include:

- a real problem;
- a method, model, system, experiment, or design to implement;
- evidence of progress, such as metrics, tests, screenshots, demo, analysis, or comparison table;
- a clear plan for how the topic can continue as your graduation project.

For AI/CV/data topics, also include a dataset or data collection plan, a baseline model, and evaluation metrics.

## Suggested Topics

### CV-01: Smart Defect Detection for Small Manufacturing

Scenario: A small factory wants to automatically detect scratches, dents, missing parts, or surface defects from camera images.

Expected result: dataset selection or small controlled dataset collection, baseline classifier or detector, precision/recall/F1 or mAP evaluation, error analysis, and demo notebook or simple web interface.

Graduation extension: larger dataset, real-time deployment, edge device inference, or segmentation-based defect localization.

### CV-02: Road Damage Detection from Street Images

Scenario: A city maintenance service wants to identify potholes and road cracks from vehicle or phone camera images.

Expected result: public dataset or collected images, detection or segmentation baseline, quantitative evaluation, visual demo with detected damage, and discussion of image-quality limitations.

Graduation extension: GPS integration, severity scoring, or mobile data collection workflow.

### CV-03: Medical Image Classification Using Public Data

Scenario: A research team wants a decision-support prototype for classifying public medical images.

Expected result: legal public dataset, transfer learning baseline, careful evaluation, comparison with a simple baseline, and clear limitation discussion without medical diagnosis claims.

Graduation extension: explainability, robustness testing, or comparison of architectures.

### CV-04: Safety Equipment Detection

Scenario: A construction company wants to monitor whether workers wear helmets or safety vests.

Expected result: object detection model, mAP evaluation, example predictions, error cases, and privacy/deployment limitation discussion.

Graduation extension: real-time video stream, edge deployment, or alert system.

### CV-05: Waste Sorting Image Classifier

Scenario: A recycling service wants to classify waste into categories such as plastic, paper, glass, metal, and organic.

Expected result: image classifier baseline, data augmentation, confusion matrix, error analysis, and simple demo interface.

Graduation extension: detection of multiple objects, mobile application, or domain adaptation.
